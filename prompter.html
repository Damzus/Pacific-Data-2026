1 · WHO I AM
LOOK: CAMERA

Poster at the TOP (title showing). Circle big (click it, press +)

Bula vinaka. My name is
Saurab Nand, and I'm representing
Fiji.

My poster is called: The
island stays. The village goes.

This project is my contribution
to telling the true story
of climate change in the
Pacific.

2 · THE IDEA
LOOK: CAMERA

Stay on the title

People often say the Pacific
is sinking.

I looked at twenty-five years
of satellite images of our
coastlines, across nine Pacific islands.

And what the data shows
is clear: that one sentence
is wrong.

3 · FOUR NUMBERS
LOOK: SCREEN

Circle small (press −). Scroll a little to the ROW OF FOUR NUMBERS. Point at each one

At the top are four
numbers.

In Solomon Islands, five islands
are already gone.

But Funafuti in Tuvalu, often
called a drowning nation, is
gaining land.

And Tarawa in Kiribati is
losing it.

4 · NINE ISLANDS
LOOK: SCREEN

Scroll to "Nine islands. Nine different answers." Point at the maps

Here are the nine islands.

Every dot is a satellite
measurement of the coast.

Red is losing land. Blue
is gaining land.

Nine islands. Nine different answers.

5 · FIJI
LOOK: SCREEN

Point at the second map: NATEWA BAY (Fiji)

This one is Fiji. Most
of this coast is red.

6 · TWO CHARTS
LOOK: SCREEN

Scroll to the TWO GREY AND COLOURED CHARTS. Point left, then right

On the left is how
fast the sea is rising:
about four to five millimetres
every year, almost the same
everywhere.

On the right is what
happened to the coast.

If the sea decided everything,
both sides would look the
same. They don't.

7 · THE PEOPLE
LOOK: SCREEN

Scroll to "Land is not the same measurement as home." Point at the MIDDLE box (Carteret)

And this is the most
important part. Land is not
the same as home.

In Papua New Guinea, the
island is growing.

But about seventeen hundred people
are being moved away, because
the water turned salty and
the gardens stopped growing.

8 · FIJI VILLAGE
LOOK: SCREEN

Point at the RIGHT box (Fiji)

And here in Fiji, in
2014, a village moved two
kilometres inland.

It was the first village
in Fiji to move because
of climate change.

9 · MY MESSAGE
LOOK: CAMERA

Scroll to the BLACK BOX at the bottom. Circle big (click it, press +)

So the message I wish
to share is this.

The sea is rising under
all of us. But what
it costs is decided island
by island.

So before we plan, we
should ask: which island? And
who lives there?
